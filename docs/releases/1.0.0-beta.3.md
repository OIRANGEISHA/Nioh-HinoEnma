# Nioh Hino-Enma 1.0.0 Beta 3

Release Owner: **OIRANGEISHA**. Channel: **Beta / Pre-release**. Target date: **2026-10-08** (Asia/Shanghai). Windows x64, verified Steam Nioh Complete Edition 1.24.8 (window 1.24.08).

This version incorporates local **0.44**, including local 0.36–0.44 after Beta 2. Beta 1 and Beta 2 remain unchanged.

## Changes / 主要变化

- Restore native hot-spring sit/wait/stand/exit using Hino-Enma's common motions. Current-pool animation, Buff and restored movement/J/I are confirmed; same-version William also stands automatically after a short wait there.
- Reported bag Lightning/Fire Talismans and Travel Amulet, repeated elemental-talisman use, and supported elemental needle/life-drain paths. The user confirmed needles, body charge and life drain during Living Weapon.
- Native Kato guardian recovery through shrine recall and death-grave pickup, followed by normal digit-9 startup and attacks.
- Native Boss HP/ki/attack/defense curves map level 1/index 1 to level 400/index 1410 linearly, rounding down. Valid saved levels 1–750 are read, mapped levels above 400 are capped, higher native scene indices and additive native growth/equipment/selected-weapon bonuses are retained.
- 温泉坐起与增益、背包护符、指定九十九招式附魔、祸斗回收及四项等级成长已整合；保留飞缘魔模型、招式和此前已确认的交互、上下梯、道场与召唤流程。

The roar retains its intrinsic status effect; an additional element is excluded. Auxiliary charge effects remain native. Life drain retains physical damage, grab and healing. Stat refresh recomputes native base plus bonuses without accumulating a previous refresh. Saved levels, points and scene indices are not changed, and current HP is not directly set.

## Validation / 验证

- Local 0.43's **499 private research checks are frozen**; local 0.44 passed **8 new hot-spring checks** with no errors/failures/skips. This is not 507 checks rerun. Private inputs/reports are excluded and are not portable CI.
- Public Beta 3 **Debug and optimized x64 Release each passed 19 C# checks and 540 payload/patch comparisons** (45 hooks × 12 layouts), with generated source exact and no game access. Pure-source/CT checks passed, including 275 unique labels and three rejection cases. The offscreen UI was reviewed without clipping.
- See [sanitized validation](../validation-beta3.json). Attached provenance identifies the actual public source commit/tree, publisher and shipped hashes. Local artifact verification and CI rebuilds are recorded separately.
- Hino-Enma wait samples were about 0.062/0.063 seconds; William's was 0.079 seconds at the current pool. Both automatically stood after short waits. Exact duration equality and the older Hino-Enma exit branch are not claimed; other pools remain pending.
- Living Weapon skill confirmation is user feedback after the observation window, not recorded per-hit damage/status measurement. Normal re-equipping/play and Kato's two recovery routes are confirmed within their scope.

## Known limitations / 已知限制

- Signpost marking is unresolved; roar plus an additional element is excluded. Needle paralysis strength, elemental/physical damage, healing totals and all airborne variants remain unmeasured/unverified.
- Actual level-up spending, new growth save/load and final ki-bar measurement remain pending. Attack scalars are not per-hit damage; trainers may override runtime values.
- Other pools/guardians, full Living Weapon protection, all item/affix/interaction/mission-save/controller variants and final weapon icon remain unverified. Digits 1–4 conflict with shortcut items; use the bag. Native shooting and actual Boss weapon switching are not implemented.
- Living Weapon retains Hino-Enma without William's full startup/weapon appearance. Guardian summons do not reproduce William's additional ki cost.
- Final public cold first-enable and direct CT enable remain pending. Use one method and one version per game process. This Beta is unsigned; provenance/attestation are not Authenticode signing or gameplay certification.
