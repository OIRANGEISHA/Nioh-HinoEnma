# Nioh Hino-Enma 1.0.0 Beta 2

Release Owner: **OIRANGEISHA**. Channel: **Beta / Pre-release**. Date: **2026-10-08** (Asia/Shanghai). Target: Windows x64, verified Steam Nioh Complete Edition 1.24.8 (window 1.24.08).

This second public Beta contains the native behavior of local **0.35**. Beta 1 remains unchanged.

## Highlights / 主要变化

- Native ladder entry, climbing up/down and exit. Both directions are user-confirmed; ladder movement no longer invokes Hino-Enma's roar, and movement/J/I/digit 5 remain normal after exit.
- Complete life-drain grab animations on the reported Yoki, Dwellers and Onryoki; every enemy/Boss is not claimed as tested.
- Digit 5 directly invokes ground/air roar, F6 purifies Yokai Realm pools, and the tool can select William or Hino-Enma for the next mission load.
- Digit 9 starts native Living Weapon reinforcement and guardian combat summons while preserving Hino-Enma moves. Guardian attacks and repeat summons, including the tested infinite-Living-Weapon case, are confirmed.
- 原生上下梯和离梯已确认；新增完整吸血、数字 5 地面／空中吼叫、F6 常世净化、数字 9 真实九十九及守护灵攻击，以及下次载入的威廉／飞缘魔切换。

## Compatibility retained / 保留与修复

- Preserve Hino-Enma's Boss model/moveset and **Boss base values plus native player growth, armor and selected melee-weapon increments**.
- Retain the confirmed reported box/door, corpse, flight-camera, bag Himorogi Fragment, Kodama, Sacred Water and revenant paths.
- Reported bag Ninja's Locks and Small Spirit Stone requests now complete native use, effects and consumption. All recovery/buff effects are not individually confirmed.
- Reported dojo locks, ki pulse, purification, melee/ranged weapon-switch and Living Weapon steps can continue. Shooting practice continues after defeating its target; this is not native ranged shooting support.
- Correct the latest reported digit 9 full-charge refusal: the native resource ID is a DWORD, while the earlier 64-bit read included unrelated adjacent padding. The fix passed offline regression; the user reconfirmed startup, guardian attack and repeat after retirement.
- 保留属性叠加与原有交互修复；背包遗发、小灵石和已反馈的道场步骤可继续。数字 1—4 仍是飞缘魔技能，道具请从背包使用。
- 最新数字 9 满槽无法启动已修正资源 ID 的读取宽度，避免误读相邻填充数据；离线回归通过，用户已再次确认启动、守护灵攻击与退场后的重复召唤正常。

## Validation / 验证

- Local 0.35: **413 Python research checks**. Portable verification covers **19 C# checks** and **480 relocation comparisons**. Python research checks depend on private verified game-code snapshots; those inputs/tests are excluded from publication and are not portable CI tests.
- Public portable verification covers **19 C# checks** and all **40 native payloads across 12 layouts** (480 comparisons). Debug/Release results and final hashes belong to the build-provenance asset for the actual published source commit.
- Public packaging changes version/UI/build metadata; the native payloads, original signatures and targets are compared with the current local adaptation.
- Gameplay evidence includes user-confirmed up/down ladder traversal and exit, movement/J/I/digit 5 after exit, reported full life-drain grabs, bag items and dojo steps, native Living Weapon reinforcement, guardian attack and repeat summon. These reports have narrower scope than whole-game validation.
- Native Living Weapon reinforcement is observed as attached. Actual guardian hit damage, the exact damage increase and full protection range have not been measured.
- The final public EXE recognized the current enabled session and all 40 native payloads using read-only inspection. Cold first-enable after a fresh game restart is not separately tested; enabled-state byte comparison and portable checks do not imply cold-injection coverage.
- 本地实测、私有研究检查与公开可移植检查分别记录；不将 413 项私有测试描述为公开 CI，也不将功能报告扩大到所有场景。

## Known limitations / 已知限制

- Digits 1–4 conflict with shortcut items. Use the bag. Not every item category, ninjutsu effect, armor/weapon affix or elemental/damage interaction has been individually verified.
- Weapon-switch teaching compatibility does not implement actual Boss weapon switching. Native shooting is not implemented. The final on-screen weapon icon still awaits explicit confirmation.
- Complete life-drain grabs and native ladder traversal are confirmed on reported targets/locations, with other enemies, Bosses and ladders still unverified.
- Living Weapon retains Hino-Enma without William's full startup animation or weapon appearance. Guardian summons do not reproduce William's additional ki cost. Other guardians, controllers and full combat/transition coverage remain unverified.
- Dialogue, shrine/script variations, all mission transitions, save/load cases and every interactable variant have not all been tested. Direct CT enabling in Cheat Engine is untested; use one method and one version per game process.
- Unsigned Windows Beta. Build provenance, checksums and any CI attestation identify origin/integrity; they do not provide Authenticode signing or certify gameplay.
- 快捷栏冲突、原生射击、实际 Boss 武器切换和完整覆盖仍未解决或验证；本版仍为未签名的预发布测试版。
